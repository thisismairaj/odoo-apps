Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads to CRM

Every lead from your Meta instant forms lands in Odoo CRM, with the campaign, ad set and ad it came from

What it does

  • New leads every 15 minutes, plus an Import leads now button. This works without a public URL, so it also runs on a local or private Odoo.
  • Real time, if you want it: point a Meta webhook at your Odoo and leads arrive within seconds. Every webhook call is checked against your app secret (X-Hub-Signature-256) before anything is read.
  • No duplicates. Each Meta lead ID is imported once, even when the webhook and the scheduled import both see it.
  • Standard answers go into the right fields: full name (or first and last name), email, phone, company, job title, street, city, zip, country and state.
  • Your own questions are kept in the lead's Notes as a readable list.
  • Marketing fields filled in: Campaign and Source (Facebook or Instagram), plus Medium (Social Media) on Odoo 20, so Odoo's CRM reports can group leads by campaign.
  • Per form settings: sales team, salesperson, tags, and whether it creates a lead or an opportunity. Switch a form off to stop importing it.

Setup

  1. At developers.facebook.com, create an app (Business type) and add the Facebook Page that runs your lead ads.
  2. Create a long-lived Page access token for that Page with these permissions: leads_retrieval, pages_manage_ads, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and ads_management.
  3. In Odoo, open CRM > Configuration > Settings > Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads, paste the Page ID and the token, and click Load forms from the Page.
  4. Open CRM > Configuration > Meta Lead Forms to choose the team, salesperson and tags for each form. Then click Import leads now, or wait for the next 15 minute run.

Real time (optional): in your Meta app, add a Page webhook with the URL shown in the settings, the same verify token, and the leadgen field, and paste the app secret into Odoo. Your Odoo must be reachable over HTTPS for this.

Good to know

  • It only reads from Meta. It never changes your forms, ads or Page.
  • The first import reaches back 30 days by default (you can change it). After that, each run asks only for leads newer than the last one imported.
  • The token and the app secret are visible to administrators only.
  • Meta access tokens can expire or be revoked. When that happens, the settings show the error from Meta and you paste a new token.
  • Depends only on CRM. Works on Odoo Community and Enterprise.
